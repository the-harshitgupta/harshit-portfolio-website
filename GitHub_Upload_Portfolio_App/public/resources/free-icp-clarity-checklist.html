ICP Clarity Checklist

A beginner-friendly worksheet to help you define who your best customer is, what they care about, and how to write a clearer marketing message.

How to use this: Spend 30-45 minutes filling this out. Do not try to sound smart. Use simple language your customer would actually say.

Step 1: Who are you trying to help?

Write the specific type of person or business you want to serve.

  • Bad: everyone, startups, business owners.
  • Better: early-stage SaaS founders selling to HR teams.

Step 2: What painful problem do they have?

List the top 3 problems they are already aware of. These should be problems they would pay to solve.

Step 3: Where do they spend time online?

Write where you can research and reach them: LinkedIn, Instagram, YouTube, Reddit, review sites, founder communities, events, or search.

Step 4: What exact words do they use?

Collect real customer language from reviews, comments, competitor pages, calls, DMs, and forums. Avoid guessing.

Step 5: Why should they choose you?

Write what makes your offer different: speed, clarity, price, process, niche focus, proof, or a stronger outcome.

Step 6: Write your simple ICP statement

Use this formula:

I help [specific customer] who struggle with [painful problem] achieve [desired outcome] using [your method].

Step 7: Your 7-day action plan

  • Day 1: Pick one customer segment.
  • Day 2: Study 5 competitors.
  • Day 3: Read 30 reviews/comments/forums.
  • Day 4: List pains, triggers, and objections.
  • Day 5: Write your ICP statement.
  • Day 6: Rewrite your homepage or offer message.
  • Day 7: Create 5 content ideas for this ICP.
